[KNOWN ISSUES CARRIED FROM VERSION 2.0 — STATUS UNCONFIRMED]
- Tri-point/single-point sample bias (see v2.0 log): model was heavily
  overfitted to tri-point inoculation samples, aggressively misclassifying
  single-point samples as "Unknown."
- Whether the v2.1 augmentation fix (synthesized single-point crops from
  tri-point images) was actually applied to this v3 dataset is NOT
  CONFIRMED — verify against the actual training pipeline before claiming
  this is resolved.

[KNOWN ISSUES FOUND IN VERSION 3 TESTING]
- Background surface bias: entire training dataset was captured on a white
  table surface. Uncertain whether the model is keying on colony texture
  or partly on background color/lighting as a classification cue.
- Plate-boundary / zoom framing bias: closer zoomed-in captures, where the
  circular petri dish boundary is no longer visible in frame, get
  misclassified as "Unknown" even for correctly in-scope species (observed
  with A. flavus). Suggests the model may be partly relying on the visible
  plate edge as a framing cue rather than purely on colony texture.

[VERSION 3.1 ROADMAP]
- Objective: reduce background/framing bias identified above through
  targeted data augmentation, without introducing new bias.
- Background variation: add a small proportion of training images captured
  on a black table surface, applied evenly across all 6 classes, so the
  model isn't over-indexed on one background color.
- Close-zoom texture crops: add a small proportion of tightly cropped,
  closer-zoom images (plate boundary not visible in frame) per class,
  applied evenly across all 6 classes, so recognition doesn't depend on
  the circular plate edge being present in the shot.
- As with the v2.1 single-point augmentation, apply both changes in equal
  proportion across every class — an uneven addition would just replace
  one bias with another rather than removing it.
